NightLock | SPHEREx Mission

Our Foray Space

Discover the SPHEREx mission and the research, technology, and discoveries that expand our view of the universe.

What we bring into view

Explore the infrared universe

NightLock makes the SPHEREx mission easier to explore through clear mission information, an interactive view of the sky, and approachable explanations of the science. Move from a broad all-sky perspective to the questions researchers are investigating across galaxies, stars, and planet-forming regions.

01 / EXPLORE

Virtual Telescope

Navigate an interactive sky interface, search for celestial objects, and explore SPHEREx observations from anywhere.

02 / SEE

102 Infrared Colors

Compare infrared wavelength bands to reveal details that visible-light views can miss, including information about temperature and composition.

03 / UNDERSTAND

Cosmic Origins

Learn how an all-sky map of galaxies helps scientists study cosmic inflation and the history of the universe.

04 / DISCOVER

Ingredients for Life

See how infrared observations help researchers investigate water ice and carbon-bearing molecules in star-forming regions.

A wider view of the cosmos

One mission. An all-sky infrared map.

SPHEREx surveys the entire sky in more than 100 infrared spectral bands. The resulting measurements give scientists a consistent way to compare billions of sources and investigate how galaxies formed, how the universe evolved, and where ingredients for life are found.
